web3vacancy is a jobs platform focused on web3 and crypto roles. The board surfaces openings from teams building across multiple ecosystems, including Tezos and Tezos-adjacent projects, so Tezos builders and contributors can find roles that match their skills. The platform is free for job seekers, includes AI-assisted CV tooling, and offers integrations so teams can syndicate openings automatically.

The site is maintained as an independent community resource and aims to lower the friction of discovering ecosystem opportunities for developers and non-developers alike.

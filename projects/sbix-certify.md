SBIX Certify provides blockchain-anchored proof of creation for digital artists and NFT creators. The problem it solves: art theft before minting. Someone downloads your art, mints it first, and on-chain they look like the original creator. You have no proof you made it first.

Certify your work BEFORE you mint. SBIX Certify creates a SHA-256 hash, adds a legal RFC-3161 timestamp, and anchors the proof on Tezos blockchain in 60 seconds. Your certificate is court-admissible evidence. Features include dual anchoring (Tezos + Aleph.im), Ed25519 signatures, professional PDF certificates with QR codes, and a public verification portal. Free tier available.
